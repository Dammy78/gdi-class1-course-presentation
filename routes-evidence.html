Westgate & Manukau — proposed route designs

Westgate and Manukau route designs (MOD07-RTE-WGT-01-PO-FREEZE and MOD07-RTE-MNK-01-PO-FREEZE) are Product Owner–approved proposed assessment routes for MOD-07. They are pending physical validation and are not operational routes or NZTA-approved routes.

Detailed route PDF packages are not published on this public site; they remain in the private NZTA submission channel.
