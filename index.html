Course introduction

The GetDrivingInstructor Class 1 Learner Licence Course is designed to help learners build knowledge, attitudes and skills for safer driving within New Zealand’s graduated driver licensing framework.

Local prototype for authorised reviewers only — not published. No assessment instruments or instructor scripts are included.

Programme structure — seven modules

MOD-01

Foundations — rules, responsibilities, licensing context.

MOD-02

Hazard awareness and risk introduction.

MOD-03

Core driving knowledge and decision-making.

MOD-04

Urban and residential driving concepts.

MOD-05

Complex environments and shared road space.

MOD-06

Progression toward restricted-licence readiness.

MOD-07

Practical on-road instruction and assessment (150 minutes).

Module detail aligns to submitted RC2-CHG-063 — not verified in this repository.

Learning outcomes

The submitted design includes nine learning outcomes (LO-1 … LO-9). Approved wording is in the NZTA submission pack — not reproduced here.

  • Licensing stages and legal responsibilities
  • Road rules in routine decisions
  • Hazard recognition and response
  • Speed and following distance
  • Intersections and shared space
  • Communication and awareness
  • Vehicle control
  • Self-reflection and risk
  • Course completion requirements

Theory component

18 hours of facilitated theory/classroom learning (frozen course design). Delivery uses multiple sessions with spacing suitable for embedding learning — exact session plan not shown in this overview.

Practical component (MOD-07)

150 minutes total practical contact in a continuous on-road session, combining:

  • Assisted instruction and coaching
  • Unassisted assessed driving — minimum 30 continuous minutes

Twelve practical competencies (PC-01 … PC-12) are assessed against the submitted competency standard — definitions not shown here.
