Seven-module learning journey

Total contact 20.5 hours: 16.0 h facilitated classroom, 2.0 h summative theory, 2.5 h MOD-07 practical. The table below matches the frozen RC2 instructor review architecture.

  1. MOD-01 — Road safety, GDLS & safe progression3.0 h · Foundations for lawful, safer progression through the graduated system.
  2. MOD-02 — Risk factors & hazard awareness3.0 h · Early hazard detection and anticipation.
  3. MOD-03 — Behaviour, decisions & influences3.0 h · Personal and social factors in driving choices.
  4. MOD-04 — Practical risk management2.75 h · Theory and preparation for on-road margins.
  5. MOD-05 — Legal responsibilities & licence conditions2.25 h · Rules, signs, and compliant habits.
  6. MOD-06 — Integration & supervised practice planning2.0 h · Integrating learning and planning practice before summatives.
  7. Summative theory2.0 h · Supervised closed-book assessment of mapped LOs.
  8. MOD-07 — Practical on-road session2.5 h · Continuous session assessing PC-01–PC-12.

Learning outcomes & competencies

Learning outcomes LO-01 through LO-09 and practical competencies PC-01 through PC-12 are mapped in the official submission pack. Summative theory emphasises LO-01, LO-02, LO-03, LO-05, LO-06, and LO-07; LO-04 is demonstrated primarily in MOD-07.
